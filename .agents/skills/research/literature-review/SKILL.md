# Academic Literature Review

This skill provides methodology for conducting comprehensive academic literature reviews including searching scholarly databases, evaluating source relevance and quality, synthesizing findings across studies, and identifying research gaps.

## Core Workflow

1. **Define scope**: Establish research question and inclusion/exclusion criteria
2. **Search databases**: Query academic databases (Google Scholar, arXiv, PubMed, etc.)
3. **Screen results**: Evaluate relevance of each source
4. **Analyze content**: Read and extract key findings from selected sources
5. **Synthesize findings**: Identify patterns, consensus, and disagreements
6. **Identify gaps**: Note areas where research is lacking

## Usage Instructions

### Input Requirements
* Research question or topic area
* Time range for literature (optional)
* Specific databases to search (optional)

### Example Prompts
* `/literature-review conduct review of papers on transformer architectures`
* `Search academic literature on this topic and summarize findings`
* "What does the research say about this subject?"
